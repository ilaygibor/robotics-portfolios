## 2025-2026: SAR2-D2

I served as a captain of SAR2-D2; the team roster appears on PDF page 2. Our portfolio documents **The Hood**, including a mecanum chassis, CAD-based fabrication, a compliant rubber-band intake, an independently controlled transfer mechanism, and a curved outtake.

The controls section describes odometry with Road Runner, asynchronous trajectories and finite state machines, PID control, and field-centric driving. The final pages cover STEM outreach and support for other FTC teams.

**Where to look:** robot overview and mechanical iteration on PDF pages 4-8; controls on pages 9-10; outreach on pages 11-12.

[Read the complete SAR2-D2 portfolio](sar2-d2-2025-2026-decode-portfolio.pdf).

## 2023-2024: RoboSapiens

As a member of RoboSapiens, I contributed the **initial intake-design sketch** reproduced and credited to me in the design-evolution section, on printed page 6 / PDF page 7. The portfolio traces our robot, **Picasso**, through claw-and-arm design, a pixel-positioning scoop, testing, and competition-driven revisions.

The team also documents encoder-based autonomous movement, webcam recognition of the team prop, driver controls, and community demonstrations. **Asher Kaynan is credited with the complete Fusion 360 CAD model** on printed page 7 / PDF page 8.

**Where to look:** robot systems on PDF pages 5-6; design evolution on pages 7-9; vision troubleshooting and material choices on page 16.
